About me

I am a sworn translator and interpreter based in Guatemala City. I work with law firms and notaries on the translation of official documents, and with institutions and organisations on liaison interpreting.

Before moving into translation full time, I taught English at the Instituto Guatemalteco Americano, and I remain active as a freelance interpreter, including work with Food For The Hungry. I am completing my degree in Translation and Interpreting at Universidad Mariano Gálvez, which lets me combine academic training with field experience.
